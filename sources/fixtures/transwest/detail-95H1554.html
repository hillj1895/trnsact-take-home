Frequently Asked Questions

What should I know before purchasing a commercial truck?

When you’re ready to buy a commercial truck, there are a few things you ought to consider. We recommend taking your time to do your research. Get to know the different types of trucks as well as the most common manufacturers. You can rely on the sales and service teams at Transwest to answer your questions on these topics. Next, make sure you know your budget. There are a variety of financing options available, so you’ll want to be sure to choose the right one for your needs. Finally, consider what maintenance you’ll need to do on your truck. For both unexpected service and regular maintenance, visit a Transwest service center near you.

What’s the best way to finance a commercial truck?

We offer programs specifically tailored to the needs of the transportation industry, and our experts are happy to discuss the various financing options for your commercial truck. Contact us today to learn more. 

Are there specialty financing programs for commercial trucks?

There are several options available for commercial trucks. From first-time buyer options to financing based on your business's growing equity, we can help. Contact us today to learn more.
